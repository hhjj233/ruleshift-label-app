    Help

    Saving

    Every click is saved in this browser at once. With a token, your answers also go to GitHub a few seconds later and whenever you change photos. The button at the top right shows the state; click it to save now. To move to another computer, open the same link there and connect with the same token.

    Done

    A photo counts as done when every question is answered. A note is needed when you choose unclear, ambiguous, cannot_tell, not_possible, other or a conflict. For photos with priority exclude, only the ego decision, the priority and a note are needed.
